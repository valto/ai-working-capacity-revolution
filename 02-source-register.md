## Source Register (Fact-Check Ledger)

Every substantive statement in every deliverable must be classifiable into exactly one of these five evidence classes (state the class inline or in a table legend):
1. OBSERVED FACT — primary documentation, official specs/pricing, filings, regulation, or a direct recording/transcript. Independently checked against a live/primary source on or immediately before 2026-08-12. Cite the exact source (URL, or for video: title, uploader/channel, publish date, platform, approximate timestamp).
2. ATTRIBUTED STATEMENT — what a named executive, organization, or source says, publicly and on the record. This is NOT automatically treated as independently proven fact, even if the fact that they said it is itself an OBSERVED FACT (e.g. "Sam Altman stated demand could grow 10x/year" is an attributed statement; the fact that he said those exact words, confirmed against a transcript, is an observed fact about the statement's existence).
3. DERIVED CALCULATION — transparent arithmetic from cited OBSERVED FACT or ATTRIBUTED STATEMENT inputs. Always show the formula, not just the result.
4. SCENARIO ASSUMPTION — a visible, editable parameter used to explore a possible case (e.g. an illustrative electricity price or utilization rate). State plainly that it is an assumption, not a market figure, and note what it controls.
5. INTERPRETATION — a clearly labelled explanation of how facts, statements, and scenarios may connect. Never presented as if it were a fact.
Never fabricate a quote, figure, or URL that was not actually found. If something cannot be verified, say so plainly and classify it as an ATTRIBUTED STATEMENT (if a real quote/claim exists but can't be independently proven true) rather than inventing corroboration. Do NOT reproduce long verbatim passages from any transcript or article — use short attributed quotes only (one sentence, rarely two), always with a citation.
