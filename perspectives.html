Who Owns Your AI

Valto Loikkanen  ·  LinkedIn  ·  2026-08-11  ·  Original — by Valto Loikkanen

Frames AI infrastructure across three layers — Compute as Infrastructure, Intelligence as Utility, and Personal Superintelligence — and argues that true digital sovereignty requires ownership of infrastructure, information, and agents, plus portability of context between providers. Source of the 'information as AI's operating system' framing used in the whitepaper.

Ownership Sovereignty Information

So, the majority agrees: the time for digital sovereignty has come, now what?

Valto Loikkanen  ·  LinkedIn  ·  2026-08-05  ·  Original — by Valto Loikkanen

Proposes a cooperative-ownership model for shared AI compute, with a published cost-sharing calculation (~€42/member/month at 50 members, ~€21/member/month at 100). The whitepaper's Global Baseline and Token-Factory workbooks reconcile this figure against a separately-computed DGX-Spark-pool cost (~€19.50-23/member/month) as two different hardware tiers, not a discrepancy.

The Overlooked AI Scale-Up Layer

Valto Loikkanen  ·  Startup Commons (Substack)  ·  2026-05-22  ·  Original — by Valto Loikkanen

Argues owner-managed SMEs — firms with existing customers, revenue, and operational knowledge — are structurally well positioned to benefit from agentic AI because the opportunity is delivery innovation, not product innovation, paired with a 'globally local' expansion pattern. Source of the whitepaper's SME/owner-manager lens (Section 38).
